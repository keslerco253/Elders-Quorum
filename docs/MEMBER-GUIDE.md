# Help

How to use My Ward Link, your ward's app. Find it under **More → Help**.

## Asking a question

Type a question in the box at the top of Help, in your own words, like "How do I
stop getting texts?" or "Where do I sign up to bring a meal?". You get a short
answer from this guide, with links to read more. Below it, every part of the
guide that mentions those words is listed. Tap a topic to jump to it.

## Getting started

- **Joining:** on the app's **Join** page, enter your name, your mobile number or
  email, and your ward's **join code** (your ward leaders share it, often as a QR
  code in a meeting or a join link). Tick the box agreeing to what the app keeps,
  then enter the 6-digit code sent to you. If a leader added you, you'll get an
  invitation instead; the first time you sign in, confirm your name and choose how
  you'd like to hear from your ward.
- **Signing in:** enter your mobile number or email and choose **Keep me signed in
  for** (just this time, 1 day, 30 days or 1 year), then **Send me a code** (in a
  ward that uses email only: enter your email and tap **Email me a sign-in link**). Type
  the 6-digit code. By email you also get a **Sign in** link; in the iPhone app,
  type the code instead (the link opens Safari). Didn't get it? Wait a minute,
  check your spam or junk folder (and Promotions in Gmail), then tap **Send a new
  code**. Only the newest code works.
- **Choose your organizations:** after joining, go to **More → My organizations**
  and tick the organizations you belong to (Elders Quorum, Relief Society, Young
  Men, Young Women, Primary, Sunday School and others in your ward).
- **Turn on notifications** so reminders and messages reach you (see
  **Notifications, texts and email**).

## Getting around

- **Tabs:** on a phone, the bottom bar has **Home**, **Lessons**, **Calendar**,
  **Plans** and **More**. **Messages** (the speech bubble, with a red number when
  something is unread) is at the top. On a computer the tabs are across the top.
  Some tabs only show if your organization uses them; **Service** is under
  **More** if it isn't a tab.
- **Switching organization or ward:** tap the name at the top left (it also has a
  shortcut to **My organizations and notifications**). Lessons and service are for
  the organization you're looking at (looking at the ward shows service from every
  organization); you can look at any organization in your ward. **Messages** and
  your Home page show everything from all of yours.
- **More** has, in order: Request a visit, My assignments, Reminders & alerts,
  Service, Messages, My Journal, Challenges, My organizations, Customize Home, Reminder
  settings, Contact & notifications, Help; then Church apps (Gospel Library and Member Tools), your
  notifications on this device (with **Turn on**), **Privacy** (Lock with Face ID,
  in the phone app), **Appearance** (Automatic, Light or Dark), your account,
  **Sign out**, **Delete my account**, and **Privacy details** and **Terms and
  conditions**. Service and Request a visit show only if an organization of
  yours uses them.
- **A red dot on More** means unread Reminders & alerts. Unread messages show on
  **Messages**.
- **📘 General Handbook links:** some pages (Lessons, Service, Request a visit) have
  a small **General Handbook** link that opens that chapter of the Church's
  General Handbook in your browser (or Gospel Library).
- **Can't find something?** Your organization may not use it (for example Plans
  or Service). Ask your leaders.

## Your organizations

**More → My organizations** lists your ward's organizations. For each one:
- **I belong to (name):** puts you on its member list, lets you write to its
  leaders and ask it for a visit, and shows its events on your Home page and
  calendar.
- **Send me its notifications:** its reminders, events and messages. Untick it to
  stop hearing from that organization (you can still look at it). If you lead an
  organization, its notifications stay on.
- **Send me ward-wide notifications (from the bishopric):** messages and events
  for the whole ward.

Then tap **Save**. Leaving an organization also takes you out of any calling and
regular teaching Sundays you had in it.

**Another ward:** if you serve in another ward too (for example a stake calling),
enter its join code under **More → My organizations → Another ward → Join ward**.
Then switch between wards at the top left.

## Notifications, texts and email

**More → Contact & notifications** decides how everything reaches you:

1. **App notifications** on each phone or computer where you turn them on (free).
2. **Also send every reminder and message by:** tick **Email** and/or **Text** to
   also get those every time.
3. **If a notification doesn't reach you:** **Text**, **Email**, **Both** or
   **Nothing**, used only when an app notification didn't reach you and neither
   box above is ticked.

Then tap **Save**. For example, for app notifications and email but no texts: turn
notifications on, tick **Email**, and leave **Text** unticked.

**When you join,** everything comes to you by the way you joined with (text or
email) and by app notification once you turn it on. Change it anytime here.

**Your phone number and email:** add, change or remove them on the same page. You
can sign in with either one, and only you and your ward's leaders can see them. A
new one is confirmed with a 6-digit code. A new email gets every reminder and message
too. When adding a phone number, **Send me reminders and messages by text** is
ticked; untick it to use the number only for sign-in codes. You can't remove your
only way to sign in. If your ward doesn't
use texting, it says "Your ward uses email".

**Don't want to use the app?** Give your ward leaders your mobile number, then text
**JOIN** to your ward's texting number (it's on the card they can give you). You'll
get a reply saying you're signed up, and from then on reminders and messages come
by text. Reply to any text to reach your leaders. Text **STOP** anytime to stop.

**Turning on app notifications:**
- **In the phone app:** More → Contact & notifications → **Turn on
  notifications** (or **Turn on** under Notifications on More, or the card on
  Home). If they're off in your phone's settings, the app offers to open Settings
  so you can turn them on there.
- **iPhone, without the app:** in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, open
  the app from your Home Screen, then turn notifications on. Until then you'll get
  email.
- **On a computer:** turn them on when asked. If it says notifications are blocked,
  allow them in your browser's settings for this site.
- **To keep notifications on screen on iPhone:** Settings → Notifications → (the
  app) → Banner Style → Persistent.

**Links in texts open the browser instead of the app?** If you have the phone app,
the link should open it. If it opens the browser and asks you to sign in, tap
**Open the app** at the top of the sign-in page (or sign in there; you'll land on
the right page either way). On iPhone you can also press and hold the link and
choose **Open in My Ward Link**; iPhone then remembers to use the app.

**Reminder settings** (**More → Reminder settings**, or 🔔 **Reminder settings** on the Lessons,
Calendar, Service and My assignments pages): for **Lessons**, **Events**,
**Service** and **Assignments** separately, choose:
- **As scheduled:** your leaders' times (for lessons, usually the Sunday evening
  before and Saturday morning; for events, usually the day before; for service,
  usually a week and a day before; for assignments you've accepted, what the
  leader who gave it set, usually the day before and the morning it's due).
- **My own reminders:** tap **+ Add a reminder** (up to 10, up to 3 by text). Pick
  when, and any mix of **App**, **Text** and **Email**. For lessons: the Sunday
  before at 7 PM, Wednesday 7 PM, Friday 7 PM, Saturday 8 AM, Saturday 7 PM or
  Sunday 7 AM. For events and service: 15 minutes to 2 weeks before. For
  assignments: when it's due, or 15 minutes to 2 weeks before (counted from its
  due time, or 9 AM if it has none).
- **No reminders.**

Your own private to-dos keep the reminders you set on each one.

Then tap **Save**. If you're teaching, you still get your teaching reminders.

**Reminders & alerts** (More): every reminder and alert sent to you in the last 30
days, in full. **Mark all as read**. Messages aren't here; they're in **Messages**.

**Stopping texts:** reply **STOP** to any text, or untick **Text** under Contact &
notifications. To get texts again, tick **Text** there and text **START** to the
app's number. Reply **HELP** for help.

**Not getting notifications?** Check that they're on for this device (More →
Contact & notifications), that they're allowed in your phone's settings, and that
you haven't unticked the organization's notifications under My organizations. If
notifications can't reach you, choose an email or text backup so you don't miss
anything. If nothing at all can reach you, your leaders are told, and may call or
see you in person instead.

## Home

Your Home page shows what's coming up in **every organization you belong to** and
the ward:
- **At the top**, anything waiting for your answer: "Would you be willing to
  accept this assignment?", "Can you take this assignment?", "Will you teach?",
  visit invitations, "Which organizations are you in?" and **Turn on
  notifications**.
- **Your assignments:** anything overdue, needing an answer, in the next two
  weeks, or with no due date.
- **This Sunday's lesson** for each organization (Sunday School's is "Come,
  Follow Me this week").
- **Coming up:** events in the next 45 days mixed in date order with your own
  dated assignments: to-dos, lessons you're teaching and service you signed up
  for. Something lasting weeks or months (like a temple open house) shows as "Now
  through …". If you'd rather see Your assignments lower down, move it with
  Customize Home.
- **Challenge**, **Ways to serve**, **Would you like a visit?** and **Church apps**.

A section with nothing in it (for example, no challenge running) doesn't show.

**Customize Home** (the link at the bottom, or More → Customize Home): move
sections up or down with ↑ / ↓, and **Hide** or **Show** them ("Conducting this
Sunday" is only for presidencies; you can ignore it). It's saved to your
account, so it's the same on every device. **Reset to the usual order** puts it
back. Things that need your answer always stay at the top.

## Lessons

- **Lessons** shows **Upcoming** and **Past** lessons for the organization you're
  looking at. Tap one to see the talk or reading (**Read the talk in Gospel
  Library**), who's teaching, and the teacher's notes once they're shared.
- **Study thoughts:** share an insight or question from your study with **Add a
  comment → Post comment**, or **Reply** to someone's. Comments appear after a
  leader approves them (yours shows "Waiting for approval" until then). You can
  delete your own.
- **Offering to teach:** on a lesson that shows **Teacher needed**, tap **I'd like
  to teach this**. Your leaders are told; **Withdraw** if your plans change.
- **Topic lessons:** some lessons have an **Assigned topic** with **Open the link
  ↗** instead of a talk.
- **Conference Sundays** say there's no lesson (General Conference Sundays link to
  the conference).
- **Sunday School** follows Come, Follow Me: each week shows that week's reading.

### When you're asked to teach

You'll get "Will you teach on Sun, Nov 1?" by notification, a card on Home, and on
the lesson page. Answer **Yes, I'll teach** or **I can't teach this one** (you can
add a reason), or text back **TEACH** or **CANT**.

### When you're teaching

- **Reminders** ("You're teaching this Sunday") ask you to confirm. Tap **Yes,
  I'm still teaching** (in the notification, email or lesson page), or text back
  **TEACH**. Your leaders see you've confirmed.
- **Can't make it?** Tap **I can't teach this lesson** on the lesson page (or text
  **CANT** and a reason). You're taken off the lesson, any notes you posted for it
  are removed, and your leaders are told. Only they see your reason.
- **Sharing notes:** on the lesson page, **You're teaching this lesson: share notes
  with everyone**. Write them and tap **Submit for approval**. After a leader
  approves them, they're on the lesson page and everyone is told. A leader may send
  them back with a note so you can edit.
- **Regular teacher:** if you're assigned a set Sunday each month (like the 2nd and
  4th), you're put on those lessons automatically; they show under My assignments.

## Calendar

- **Calendar** shows a month of events from your ward and organizations. Use the
  chips at the top to see one organization. Tap a day to see its events. Events that
  last weeks or months show as **Going on: …** at the top.
- **An event's page:** the time (or "All day"), place (tap for a map), details, flyer, **Add to
  Google Calendar**, **Add to Apple / Outlook calendar** and **Share**.
- **RSVP:** if asked, tap **Going**, **Maybe** or **Can't go**.
- **Your own events:** tap **+ My event** (or pick a day, then **+ My event this
  day**) to put something of yours on your calendar, like an appointment: title,
  date, **Starts**, **Ends** (optional), where and notes. For something with no
  time (a birthday, a day off), tick **All day** instead; its reminders count from
  9 AM that day. **Only you can see it**
  (marked **🔒 Only you**), on your calendar and Home. Add reminders the same way
  as on your to-dos (when it starts, or 15 minutes to 2 weeks before, by **App**
  and/or **Email**), then tap **Add to my calendar**. Tap it later to change or
  delete it. It's deleted 30 days after its date. If you've subscribed to the
  calendar (below), your own events show there too.
- **Meetings you're invited to** (like an activities committee): you get an
  invitation, and the meeting shows on your calendar and Home's Coming up marked
  **Invited**. Open it to see when and where, the agenda, who's giving the
  prayers and spiritual thought, and who's invited, and tap **I'll be there** or
  **I can't come** (it says **Please respond** until you do). From the calendar,
  tap **See the agenda and answer**; **Add it to your calendar** puts it in your
  own calendar app. You get a reminder before it starts unless you said you can't
  come.
- **Subscribe:** **Calendar → Subscribe** adds your ward's calendar to Apple
  Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook once; events then appear and stay up to date
  on their own. Choose a **Reminder** and **Second reminder** for every event and tap
  **Save reminders** (Apple Calendar and Outlook use them; Google Calendar doesn't,
  so set reminders there, and it checks for new events only a few times a day). On
  iPhone, turn off **Remove Alerts** when subscribing. To stop, delete the calendar
  in your calendar app. Your link is just for you: if you shared it by mistake, tap
  **Reset my link** (calendars using the old link stop updating). Under **What's
  in your calendar**, choose **Both** (the usual), **Ward and organization
  events** or **My own events**, and tap **Save**. Change it anytime; the same link
  keeps working and your calendar app shows the change the next time it checks.
  Keep the link to yourself if it has your own events.
- **Suggest an event:** **Request an event** on the Calendar (if your organization
  takes suggestions): what it is, when, where and details. You're told when it's
  approved or declined.

## Service

- **Service** lists ways to help: projects, meals, rides and more. Tap one to see
  its spots, how many people each needs, and who has signed up.
- **Sign up:** add an optional note and tap **Sign me up** on a spot. You can take
  more than one. Your note shows next to your name for everyone who opens it. To
  cancel, tap **✕ Remove** next to your name. "Signing up for this is closed" means
  it's no longer taking sign-ups. **Share** sends it to someone.
- **Reminders:** you get reminders before it, as set by your leaders (usually a
  week and a day before), or as you chose under Reminder settings.
- **Friends without the app** can sign up from a public link your leaders share, if
  there is one.

## Messages

Tap **Messages** at the top (the speech bubble on a phone).
- **Inbox:** your conversations with your leaders, and announcements from your
  ward and organizations, newest first. A blue dot means unread. Tap an
  announcement to read it in full; you can reply to its leaders below it (only they
  see your reply).
- **Writing to your leaders:** tap **New message** and choose who it's **To**: the
  whole presidency (or bishopric), **Only** one leader (private: the rest don't see
  it), or a group you can send to. You can write to the leaders of organizations you
  belong to, and to the bishopric.
- **Conversations** read like a text thread. Under your last message it says
  **Delivered**, then **Seen** once a leader reads it.
- **Sent** lists what you've written.
- **By text:** if you get texts, you can just reply to one; it goes to your leaders.
  If two organizations texted you recently, you'll be asked which your message is
  for: reply with the number. Replying to an **email** doesn't reach anyone; use its
  button to open the app.
- **Private messages:** notifications for a private message say only who it's
  from; open the app to read it.
- **No New message button?** Your ward or organization may use the app only to
  send information out. Contact your leaders another way.

## My assignments and to-dos

**More → My assignments** brings together everything on your plate:
- 📋 **Assignments** from a presidency or bishopric;
- 📝 **your own to-dos**;
- 📖 **lessons you're teaching**;
- 👥 **service you signed up for**.

**Coming up** and **Done and past** tabs. Tap one to open it.

**When you're given an assignment,** you're asked "Would you be willing to accept
this assignment?" by notification and a card on Home. Read the details (due date,
time and any attachment) and tap **Yes, I accept** or **I'm not able to** (you can
add a note). After you accept, you get its reminders before it's due.

**On an assignment:** add **progress notes** and set it to **Not started**, **In
progress** or **Done**; your leaders are told. If something changes, tap **I'm
unable to do this assignment** (with an optional note): your leaders are told and
it comes off your list. If you're asked "Can you take this assignment?", answer
**Yes, I'll take it** or **I can't**.

**Your own to-dos:** tap **+ My to-do** for a private to-do with a title, details,
a due date and time, and reminders. **Only you can see it**: no one else, not even
your leaders. Tap **+ Add a reminder** for each one you want: when it's due, or 15
minutes to 2 weeks before. A new to-do starts with reminders 1 day and 1 hour
before; change them or remove one with ✕, then tap **Add to-do**. Without a time,
they count from 9 AM that day. Under each reminder, tick how it comes: **App** (a
notification on your phone or computer) and/or **Email**. With neither ticked it
comes as an app notification (or by email if notifications are off). No email
box? Add an email under More → Contact & notifications. Tap a to-do to edit it, mark it done (**✓ Mark done**;
**Not done yet** undoes it) or delete it. Done to-dos are deleted after 6 months.

## My Journal

**More → My Journal** opens today's page with the date at the top. Write in the
box: it **saves as you type** ("Saved ✓" shows under it). Write as many entries
in a day as you like: after the first, tap **+ Start another entry** (each shows
the time you wrote it). Tap an entry to change it, or **Delete entry**.

- **Other days:** **←** and **→** go a day back or forward; **Go to a day** jumps
  to a date; **Back to today** returns.
- **📖 All entries:** everything you've written, newest first by month, with a
  **Search** box. Tap one to open its day.
- **🖨️ Print / Export:** choose **The whole journal**, **This year** or **Days I
  choose**, then **Print or save as PDF** (each day is a heading, with what you
  wrote and when) or **Download Word document** to keep, edit or back up. In the
  phone app, printing opens your phone's print screen and the download opens the
  share sheet (Save to Files, email and so on).
- **Private:** only you can see your journal. Your leaders and the site owner
  can't, and it's stored encrypted.
- **Passcode (optional):** tap **Add a passcode** at the bottom of My Journal. Your
  journal then asks for it when you open it and locks again after 30 minutes
  without using it (or tap **Lock now**). What you write is locked with your
  passcode, which isn't kept anywhere. **If you forget it, no one can reset it**:
  the only way back is **Forgot your passcode? → Erase my journal and start
  over**. Change it or turn it off under **Change or turn off passcode**.
- **Keep a copy:** deleting your account (or not signing in for 12 months)
  deletes your journal too, so download it from time to time.

## Visits

**Request a visit** (**Home → Would you like a visit?** or **More → Request a
visit**): choose who you'd like to hear from (one of your organizations'
presidencies, or the bishopric), and **A visit in my home**, **A chance to talk
(phone or at church)** or **Help with something**. Add the best days or times and
anything you'd like them to know (only those leaders see it), then **Send
request**. You'll see its status and any reply there, and can **Cancel request**.
You can have one open request with each.

**When you're invited to a visit,** you get a personal invitation with the date and
time. Answer **Yes, that works for us**, **Suggest a different date or time**, or
**Now is not a convenient time for us**, from the notification, email, text link or
the card on Home. You'll get a friendly reminder before an agreed visit; reply if
something comes up.

## Plans

**Plans** (if your organization uses them) has pages your leaders keep for ward
and organization plans, like a youth temple trip or a service project, with
details, pictures and PDFs you can read right on the page. You'll be told when a
plan is posted or updated, if your leaders choose.

## Challenges

A friendly competition between organizations, like who does more temple and family
history work. Open it from **More → Challenges** or the card on Home while it's on.
If you're on a team, tap **+ Log it**, choose what you did and how many. Only team
totals are shown; **only you see what you logged**, and you can remove your own
entries while it's running. Everyone on the teams hears the final scores.

## The phone app

The iPhone and Android app does everything the website does, plus:
- **Face ID lock:** More → Privacy → **Lock with Face ID** (or fingerprint). It
  asks when the app opens, and again if you come back after more than a minute
  away; tap **Unlock** if the prompt closes.
- **Home-screen widget:** this Sunday's lesson, this week's Come, Follow Me
  reading, your next event, and unread messages. On iPhone, add it from the Home
  Screen's widgets (iOS 17 or later); on Android, long-press the home screen →
  Widgets → My Ward Link.
- Pull down to refresh a page, and tap the tab you're on to scroll to the top. If
  you're offline it says "Can't reach My Ward Link"; tap **Try again**.
- A number on the app icon for unread messages and alerts; links in emails open in
  the app; texted sign-in codes appear above the keyboard.

## Your account and privacy

- **Your phone and email:** More → Contact & notifications. Your name is the one
  you gave when you joined (or that your leaders entered).
- **Appearance:** More → Appearance: Automatic, Light or Dark.
- **Sign out:** More → **Sign out** (it also stops notifications on that device).
- **Delete my account:** More → **Delete my account**, type DELETE. This removes
  your name, contact info, comments, sign-ups, your journal and everything you
  posted (download your journal first to keep it). You can
  join again later with the join code. Without the app, go to
  **mywardlink.net/delete-account**, sign in with a code, and delete it there (it
  also lists exactly what's deleted and what's kept).
- **What the app keeps:** your name, phone or email, and what you post. It never
  uses Church records, addresses or birth dates. Old information is deleted
  automatically (for example, sign-ups 30 days after the event, conversations with
  leaders after 6 months, announcements after a year), and accounts not used for 12
  months are deleted. See **Privacy details** under More.
